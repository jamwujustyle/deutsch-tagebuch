# Lektion 09 — Sentence structure, part 1 — English

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6db5VSUm2o

Rules from the supplied slide; the practice sentences are added examples. Explain each rule, then translate the sentences.

## Subject and verb agreement

1. What determines the form of the conjugated verb?
2. I live in Berlin.
3. You live in Berlin. (informal singular)

## Statements

1. Where does the conjugated verb go in a simple statement? Does a position always mean one word?
2. I am learning German.
3. This morning I am learning German. (Start with “this morning”.)

## Questions with question words

1. Where does the conjugated verb go in a question beginning with a question word?
2. Where do you live? (informal singular)
3. Where do you come from? (formal)

## Yes/no questions

1. Where does the conjugated verb go in a yes/no question?
2. Do you live in Berlin? (informal singular)
3. Do you speak German? (formal)
