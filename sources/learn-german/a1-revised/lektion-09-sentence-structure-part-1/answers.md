# Lektion 09 — Sentence structure, part 1 — German and explanations

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6db5VSUm2o

Rules from the supplied slide, clarified below; the practice sentences are added examples.

## Subject and verb agreement

1. The subject determines the verb form: the conjugated verb agrees with the subject in person and number.
2. Ich **wohne** in Berlin. — Subject: **ich**; verb form: **wohne**.
3. Du **wohnst** in Berlin. — Subject: **du**; verb form: **wohnst**.

## Statements

1. In a simple statement, the conjugated verb takes **position 2**. A position is one sentence unit, which can contain several words. If another unit comes first, the subject follows the conjugated verb.
2. Ich **lerne** Deutsch. — Position 1: **Ich**; position 2: **lerne**.
3. Heute Morgen **lerne** ich Deutsch. — Position 1: **Heute Morgen** (two words, one unit); position 2: **lerne**; the subject **ich** follows the verb.

## Questions with question words

1. In a basic **W-Frage**, the question word or phrase comes first, followed by the conjugated verb in **position 2**. In the questions below, the subject follows the verb. **Wo?** means “Where?”; **Woher?** means “Where from?”
2. Wo **wohnst** du? — **Wo** → **wohnst** → **du**.
3. Woher **kommen** Sie? — **Woher** → **kommen** → **Sie**.

## Yes/no questions

1. In a basic **Ja/Nein-Frage**, the conjugated verb takes **position 1**, followed by the subject. The answer can begin with **ja** (yes) or **nein** (no).
2. **Wohnst** du in Berlin? — **Wohnst** → **du** → **in Berlin**.
3. **Sprechen** Sie Deutsch? — **Sprechen** → **Sie** → **Deutsch**.
